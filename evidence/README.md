# Evidence ledger

Record the material that can move an idea up or down.

Required fields:

- source or artifact;
- exact claim supported;
- method of checking;
- limitations;
- independent replication status;
- consequence for the idea;
- date and contributor.

Negative results are first-class evidence.

## Reproducible runs

- `runs/network-observation-v0.1.json` — exact enumeration of 16 four-bit
  worlds under central, distributed, and partial sensor configurations on
  cliques and paths. Generated by
  `python3 -m network_relativity.experiment --output evidence/runs/network-observation-v0.1.json`.
- `network-observation-v0.1.md` — interpretation and claim boundary for that
  machine-readable receipt.
- `runs/network-observation-v0.2.json` — the same 12 finite conditions with
  root-relative routes, rounds, messages, and bit-transmission costs.
- `network-communication-v0.2.md` — interpretation and claim boundary for the
  communication-cost extension.
- `runs/network-observation-v0.3.json` — all-world certificate coverage,
  minimal attested-sample counts, and logical payload bounds for the same 12

- `network-certificates-v0.3.md` — interpretation, negative results, and the
  explicit identity/honesty trust boundary for source-grounded attestations.
- `runs/network-observation-v0.4.json` — exhaustive static-world temporal
  profiles for rotating sensing, root-link failure, and connectivity recovery.
- `network-dynamics-v0.4.md` — temporal contract, all-world outcomes, and the

- `runs/network-observation-v0.5.json` — exhaustive prospective-model,

- `timespace-v0.5.md` — frozen timespace model, predictions, executed outcomes,
  negative control, and consent boundary.
- `runs/network-observation-v0.6.json` — six frozen trajectory/envelope cases
  plus the endpoint-only acceleration blind-spot probe.
- `trajectory-compatibility-v0.6.md` — trajectory definitions, executed
  outcomes, exact accepted witnesses, misses, and the physical-energy boundary.
- `runs/network-observation-v0.7.json` — correction lifecycle profiles,

- `theorem-ledger-v0.7.md` — classification of the instrument's statements as

- `community-archive-network-substrate-v0.1.md` — aggregate source inventory,
